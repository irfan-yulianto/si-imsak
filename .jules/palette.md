## 2024-05-14 - Accessible Disabled Button State
**Learning:** Native `disabled` buttons lose their focusability and tooltips. This is problematic for users with screen readers or those who rely on keyboard navigation, as they cannot access the tooltip to understand *why* the button is disabled. In this app, many icon buttons don't have text and might rely on standard tooltips or accessible explanations to convey disabled state.
**Action:** Replace `disabled={disabled}` with `aria-disabled={disabled}`, conditionally styling it using `aria-disabled:cursor-not-allowed aria-disabled:opacity-40` instead of `disabled:cursor-not-allowed disabled:opacity-40` and manually overriding `onClick` and `onKeyDown` behavior. Add a `title` attribute matching the `aria-label` for icon buttons, so that mouse users see a tooltip describing what the icon-only button does.
