## 2024-03-24 - Accessibility for Disabled Buttons
**Learning:** Native `disabled` attribute on buttons removes them from the tab order and prevents hover events, making them inaccessible to keyboard users and preventing native tooltips from showing. This is particularly problematic for icon-only buttons that rely on `title` or tooltip on hover/focus to explain why they are disabled, or general form buttons. Using `aria-disabled="true"` instead preserves focusability and tooltips while semantically indicating the disabled state to screen readers.
**Action:** Replace `disabled` with `aria-disabled="true"` on custom Button components and handle click prevention manually in the `onClick` handler. Update tests that check for `toBeDisabled()` to check for the `aria-disabled` attribute instead.
